# Cutout photo stickers

These are photographs, manually masked and processed with deterministic grain, a cream paper outline, desaturation, resizing, and WebP compression. No generated imagery is used.

- `camera.webp`: Raphael.bosshard, Canon AE-1, released into the public domain. [Original and rights statement](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Canon-ae1.jpg).
- `lens.webp`: USDA Agricultural Research Service photograph, transparent crop by BradBeattie, public domain. [Original and rights statement](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Large_format_camera_lens.png).
- `film.webp`: Jakob Owens, *Film — A Dying Art*, published on Unsplash in April 2017 under CC0. [Original and rights statement](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Film_-_A_Dying_Art_(Unsplash).jpg).

The optimized images are embedded in `cutout.html` so copying or downloading the component does not require a separate assets folder. Rebuild them with `node scripts/prepare-cutout-stickers.mjs`.
